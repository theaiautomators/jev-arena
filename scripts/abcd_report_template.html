Audit and verification

What the audit can establish

Gold and model identity are hidden from the judge. Identical case/answer pairs share a review. The 76-question selection was frozen independently of outcomes. Disagreements are inspected without automatically changing references. Automated review is not human semantic validation or a population error-rate estimate.

What was independently checked

All 12,720 unique records, input hashes, frozen source hashes, 17 upstream files, reconstruction of all 2,400 natural inputs and 300 conversation alignments, native capacity decisions, combined scores, live cost ledgers and local cleanup. Failed and unsupported attempts remain visible. Scenario, target and future text were excluded from candidate inputs.

Scope and limitations

This is an offline next-step/action-label adaptation of ABCD, not full published AST, action-slot accuracy, generated support responses, full Cascading Dialogue Success or live customer-resolution success. Bootstrap intervals cluster by conversation and are descriptive, unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Rare-label support is small. No model was fine-tuned for this assessment.
